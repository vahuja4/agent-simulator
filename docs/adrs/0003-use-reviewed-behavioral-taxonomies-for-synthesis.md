# Use reviewed behavioral taxonomies for scenario synthesis

Phase 4.5 uses exactly four mutually exclusive Persona archetypes—Cooperative, Pressure, Vigilant, and Persistent—and treats Knowledge level as an independent low/medium/high coverage axis. Every Scenario has exactly one primary archetype and no secondary archetypes, and Same-cell equivalence compares that single value. A Persona archetype is admitted only when it can plausibly cause a distinct agent failure; synonyms and merely cosmetic temperaments do not qualify. The Pressure archetype references the confirmation-gate behavior in `docs/solutions/simulator-model-luna.md` rather than restating it. Pressure attempts to rush past a gate for an in-scope action the agent is willing to perform, creating confirmation-skip risk; Persistent re-attempts after refusal or an unsatisfactory answer, creating wear-down-compliance or repetitive-loop risk. In short, Pressure is speed against procedure, while Persistent is attrition against refusal.

Every curated scenario persona is mapped to exactly one reviewed archetype in `docs/reports/persona-archetype-mapping.md`. A mismatch reveals either a missing archetype or Persona/Complication fusion that must be resolved. Terseness is surface style, confused-customer behavior belongs to low Knowledge level, and requests to verify an outcome belong to Goal and success criteria rather than Persona identity.

The prototype's taxonomy is corrected at the domain boundary: changing one's mind once is the mid-conversation-correction Complication, while deliberately answering only as asked or partially disclosing required facts is the underspecification Complication rather than a Persona archetype. Knowledge level is independent because terminology and factual fluency create failure modes distinct from temperament. Under the Sealed-world rule, low knowledge may use an incorrect term for a real fixture fact, but no level may invent facts.

Synthesized Coverage cells contain zero or one Complication, and zero-Complication baselines are mandatory controls for both agent behavior and harness false alarms. Compound complications are never synthesized. Revisit a particular compound only when a Phase 5 real-agent failure demonstrates an interaction effect that no single-Complication scenario reproduces; that combination is then hand-designed with its own success criteria.
