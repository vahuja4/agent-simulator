# Use a relevant-fluency ladder for Knowledge level

Phase 4.5 defines Knowledge level by observable relevant fluency rather than
surface vocabulary or journey-specific taxonomies. **Low** must exhibit at
least one material fluency gap: a wrong label for a real fact or a relevant
concept that needs explanation. **Medium** must state Goal-relevant facts
correctly and visibly rely on the agent for at least one procedural rule or
consequence. **High** must make at least one correct, unprompted statement of a
relevant rule or consequence. Simulator compliance checks this evidence on
every fitness repetition; identical behavior can never earn credit for more
than one level.

The Sealed-world rule applies symmetrically. Knowledge level changes fluency,
not the available factual universe: no level may invent Fixture state, domain
rules, or tool internals. Knowledge level also does not control disclosure
timing, which belongs to the Complication contract, or Persona behavior.

Knowledge level and false premise are orthogonal. Knowledge governs fluency;
the false-premise Complication governs an incorrect belief about real Fixture
state. A high-knowledge, false-premise Scenario—a confident but wrong
customer—is therefore eligible and valuable; false premise never implies low
knowledge.

Level pairings are eligible by default. A particular realization is
non-applicable only when the Fixture and represented domain rules lack a real,
behaviorally distinguishable referent with which to demonstrate its required
level evidence. Missing generator support is BLOCKED, not an exclusion.

Low-knowledge wrong-label realizations are new input to judge criteria that
were calibrated on correct-term Personas. The first realization at low level
is therefore also a judge-robustness test, with the same
**designed, not yet empirically validated** status as an unpopulated
Complication. Any judge instability is recorded as an N-series entry and
resolved through the calibration-gated process; criterion wording is not
changed without explicit approval and live verification.
