# Productize the existing scenario-synthesis prototype

Phase 4.5 will audit and evolve the committed `scenario_synthesis/` prototype rather than build a parallel system or start over. Its deterministic J1 blueprint machinery is the baseline, but all previously generated candidates remain quarantined and must pass the newly agreed fitness checks from scratch before they can enter the scenario library; historical realization or dry-run results confer no fitness credit. The earlier synthesis plans and phase notes remain historical evidence, not the Phase 4.5 specification.
