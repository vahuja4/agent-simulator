# Vishal surfaced the perturbation/complication split the course had missed

Vishal correctly pointed out the repo has both perturbations AND complications
— the course (lesson 0002, curveball catalog) had covered only perturbations.
This was a gap in the teaching, not in his understanding: he knew the codebase
vocabulary better than the materials did.

Established: perturbations = journey/data mechanics (6 types, graph edges,
phase-4.1 layer); complications = conversational difficulty (9 closed values,
ADR-0005, contracts/complication-applicability.yaml, phase-4.5 coverage
planner layer). Boundary rule is written in ADR-0005.

**Evidence**: he initiated the distinction himself, unprompted.

**Implications**: (1) the newer phase-4.5 synthesis layer (generator/planner/
candidate/contracts/ledger/qualification) is largely untaught and should get
its own lesson — it is where current development lives (phase-4.5 handoff);
(2) check future materials against BOTH taxonomies; (3) he reads the repo
independently between sessions — materials must keep up with the actual tree,
not the subset already covered.
