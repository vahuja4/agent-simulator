The Complication Taxonomy

The second closed difficulty axis — not to be confused with perturbations. Source: scenario_synthesis/contracts/complication-applicability.yaml, governed by docs/adrs/0005-use-a-closed-complication-taxonomy.md.

The boundary rule (ADR-0005, verbatim)

"Procedure branches, validation outcomes, tool failures, and fixture conditions are not conversational Complications." — Perturbation = journey/data mechanics (what happens in the flow). Complication = conversational difficulty (how the customer communicates, or what they wrongly believe).

Rules

  • Exactly nine values, closed. Every scenario carries exactly one; synthesized scenarios use at most one non-none.
  • Applicability is a reviewed precondition matrix — each value names the journey edges, events, and fixture predicates it requires; missing semantic support is a pair-eligibility exclusion, missing implementation support is BLOCKED.
  • Sharp discriminators are written down: correction preserves the instruction, goal-shift replaces it ("parameter count is never the discriminator"); a J1 "complete payment instruction" is itself the definition of a Goal.
  • Hash-pinned to the journey graph, fixtures, and the curated mapping doc (docs/reports/complication-taxonomy-mapping.md) — same drift-guard pattern as everything else.
  • Where it lives: the complication field on the coverage-planner blueprints (generator.py / planner.py, the phase-4.5 synthesis layer), one axis among several (persona-archetype × complication × …). The simulator's system prompt honors it ("even when your persona or complication calls for pressure").
